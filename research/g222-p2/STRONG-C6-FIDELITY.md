# G2.22 C6 — Strong Bridge and Source Fidelity Audit

**Research status:** Synthetic finite countermodel; the source author has not approved either interpretation. G2.21 B0–B6 remains NO RUN. This is NOT a transcribed participant theory. No historical raw dialogue or copyrighted PDF bytes are copied to the repository.

## Shared positive commitments
M = rule-object is in its own application domain; D = rule-object is defined; K = an agent remains committed to the rule. Every tested bridge **retains M=D=K=true**.

The common rule states: defined objects in the domain do not possess an object-level property A (Absolute-as-truth-predicate).

1. **Collapsed bridge (B0):** add researcher axiom K -> A. Then M,D imply NOT A and K implies A. No classical satisfying assignment.
2. **Separated bridge (B1):** keep K and A as distinct sorts/roles. Witness M=D=K=true, A=false satisfies all shared conditions.
3. **Equivalent bridge (B2):** add K iff A. Again no satisfying assignment.

Sixteen valuations of the four Boolean atoms are enumerated independently in JavaScript and Python. Result B0=0, B1=1, B2=0 admissible worlds. Unlike the prior weak C6 models, **none** of M,D,K is dropped.

2. Both commitment-use and predicate-use of 'absolute' must remain live alternatives.
3. Compare alternatives on the same domain/self-reference and definedness assumptions.
4. Do not infer the metaphysics of freedom/nonfinalization from a classical logical gap.

7. A rival may not win by removing the fixed/mutable tension it is supposed to explain.
8. Count bridge axioms as added commitments; match formal logic/horizon/resources across candidates.
